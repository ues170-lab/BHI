Positioning

Identity is the battlefield.

Identity-centered recovery practice for people the standard model misses — built from lived experience, clinical discipline, and a refusal to sell a polished ending.

Educational resource — not a treatment provider

What this is

An identity-centered curriculum project for counselors, programs, and reentry work — built on the idea that changing behavior without changing self-concept rarely holds.

What this isn't

A treatment provider, a crisis service, or a source of diagnosis. This site does not establish a counselor-client relationship.

Who it's for

Counselors and programs working with people the standard model struggles to reach — the chronic relapser, the labeled client, anyone who already knows the usual script falls short.

Where it comes from

Built on lived experience and clinical practice, and grounded in the memoir Broken But Breathing.

Educational resource. Not a treatment provider. Nothing on this site is diagnosis, individualized treatment, medical advice, or emergency care.
