Broken Halo Institute is an identity-centered recovery practice project built for counselors, programs, and people the standard model often misses. It brings lived experience, clinical discipline, harm reduction, and direct language into one controlled public platform.

What this is

A professional foundation, not a fake finished institute.

This site is intentionally restrained. It explains the model, the boundaries, and the book behind it without pretending the full curriculum library has already completed review and pilot steps.

Behavior change matters. The Institute starts from a harder claim: when the verdict someone lives under stays untouched, skills alone often do not hold.

Philosophy

What the model argues

Most recovery work addresses behavior first: stop using, follow structure, learn coping skills, reduce risk. That work matters. The Institute exists because behavior change without identity change often collapses under the weight of an older verdict.

The working stance is direct but bounded: accountability without humiliation, harm reduction without pretending the setting is safe, and confrontation aimed at the pattern rather than the person. Nothing here is diagnosis, treatment, or crisis care.

About

Who is behind it

Eric Lee Sanders is a California-certified substance-use counselor, SUDCC II, and founder of Broken Halo Institute. The work draws from counseling practice, leadership experience, harm-reduction exposure, and lived experience with addiction, incarceration, relapse, and rebuilding.

The Institute is kept separate from Broken Halo Co. on purpose. The company carries story, brand, and merch; the Institute carries doctrine, educational tools, and future growth into training and program-development work.
