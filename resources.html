Guilt & Shame Processing Worksheet

Guilt tells you what you did. Shame tells you who you think you are. This five-page worksheet helps separate facts from the shame story, identify what is inside and outside your control, and move toward accountability without identity collapse.

Identity Work Handout

The identity I inherited, built, and can rebuild. Your past explains parts of you; it does not get final authority over you. This printable handout walks through the label you carry, what that identity protected, and the daily practice that builds new evidence.
